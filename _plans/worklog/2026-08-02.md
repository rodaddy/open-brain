  local-clone deploy reported success while the old process still answered

  `.deployed-revision` == shipped sha, BEFORE health.

  wrapper command in `$W` produced exit-127 "no such file" across all 10 gate
  probes — looked like a broken swap, was the probe. Write commands out.
